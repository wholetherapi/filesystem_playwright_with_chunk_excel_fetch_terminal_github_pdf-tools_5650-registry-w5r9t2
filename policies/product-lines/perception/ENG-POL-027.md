# ENG-POL-027 — Perception & Sensor Fusion Product-Line Review Standard

- **Level:** Product line
- **Owner:** Perception & Sensor Fusion Engineering
- **Scope:** The Perception & Sensor Fusion product line only
- **Status:** Active

## 1. Applicability

This standard applies to all releases owned by the Perception & Sensor Fusion product
line. It refines the division and network standards for this product line.

## 2. Minimum

Release teams must document at least **72 hours** of independent verification review
per major release.

## 3. Observed practice

In recent certification cycles, successful releases documented 78–96 hours of review.
This observed range describes actual outcomes and is not itself a stated minimum.

## 4. Architecture conformance

The perception backbone shall conform to architecture standard **KS-STD-0331**. Release
records must name the upstream reference implementation used for the backbone.

## 5. Evidence retention

Review logs, reviewer attestations, and architecture conformance notes are retained for
seven years in the release quality archive.
