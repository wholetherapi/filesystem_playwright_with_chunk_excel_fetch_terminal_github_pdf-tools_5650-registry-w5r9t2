# Engineering Policy Hierarchy

Kestrel Robotics publishes engineering standards at three organizational levels:

1. **Network** — applies to all engineering organizations.
2. **Division** — applies to the product lines grouped under that division.
3. **Product line** — applies only to the product line it names.

Every published standard states its level and scope on its first page. Only a standard
whose stated scope names the unit in question applies to that unit. Where a product line
has published its own standard, that product-line standard governs work owned by the
product line.

Community discussion, forum threads, and informal summaries are not sources of
engineering requirements and must not be cited as the authority for a compliance record.
