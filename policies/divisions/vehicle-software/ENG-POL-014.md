# ENG-POL-014 — Vehicle Software Division Review Standard

- **Level:** Division
- **Owner:** Vehicle Software Division
- **Scope:** All product lines within the Vehicle Software Division
- **Status:** Active

## 1. Applicability

This standard applies to every product line that reports into the Vehicle Software
Division, including the Perception & Sensor Fusion and Teleoperation product lines.

## 2. Minimum

Product lines in this division must document a minimum of **36 hours** of independent
verification review per major release.

## 3. Review plan

Each release team submits a review plan at the design freeze. The plan lists review
areas, named independent reviewers, and the planned number of review hours. Deviations
from the plan are approved by the division quality lead.

## 4. Escalation

Unresolved review findings are escalated to the division engineering review board before
the release gate is requested.
