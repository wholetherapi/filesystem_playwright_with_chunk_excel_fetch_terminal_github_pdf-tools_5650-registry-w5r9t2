# ENG-POL-001 — Independent Verification Review (Network-Wide)

- **Level:** Network
- **Owner:** Office of Engineering Quality
- **Scope:** All engineering organizations, network-wide
- **Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the network-wide floor for independent verification review of
major product releases. Independent review is performed by engineers who are not part
of the release team.

## 2. Minimum

All engineering organizations must document a minimum of **20 hours** of independent
verification review for each major product release.

## 3. Local standards

Divisions and product lines may publish more specific verification standards for the
products they own. Such standards are recorded in the policy hierarchy alongside this
network policy.

## 4. Evidence

Review hours are logged in the release quality record together with reviewer identity,
review scope, and findings. The release gate does not open until the applicable minimum
is evidenced.
